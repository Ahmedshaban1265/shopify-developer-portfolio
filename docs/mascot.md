# VEQRU developer mascot

The hero uses an original AI-generated studio mascot, created on 11 November
2026 from the owner's visual reference and description. It is an illustration,
not a portrait of either founder.

The final version incorporates the owner's refinement: a slightly slimmer
torso and arms, with a small natural belly. The same face, beard, hoodie,
seated pose, laptop, code panel and palette were retained.

## Website assets

- `public/images/veqru-developer-640.webp`
- `public/images/veqru-developer-1100.webp`

Both are square WebP files with transparent alpha. `content/site.js` supplies
the responsive image sources, and the two locale files supply its alt text.

## Character direction

Friendly male developer with light warm skin, short dark hair, a neat full
beard and a relaxed smile. A comfortable charcoal hoodie with mint lining and
drawstrings, sage trousers and off-white sneakers. Seated on a rounded sage
beanbag, using a dark laptop with a small floating code panel. Soft matte 3D
clay materials and rounded proportions. The palette follows VEQRU's charcoal,
mint, sage, ivory and steel-blue tokens. Keep the full figure within the frame
on a transparent background.

## Final refinement prompt

Edit this exact VEQRU developer mascot. Make only a subtle body-build adjustment:
the man should be approximately 15 percent slimmer through his belly, torso and
upper arms, while retaining a small natural rounded belly and a comfortable
hoodie. He must still be stocky and friendly, not thin, muscular or athletic.
Keep the same identity, face, skin, beard, hairstyle, clothing, laptop, floating
code panel, beanbag, seated pose, 3D clay material, lighting, framing and palette.
Do not redesign or add elements. Preserve the square canvas, complete figure
and genuinely transparent alpha background.

Generation and refinement used the built-in image generator. The website copies
were encoded as WebP at two widths, preserving the alpha channel.
